# Payment innovation radar
1. Measure customer finish-to-confirmation and merchant settlement separately. Faster bank rails do not automatically mean faster UX.
2. Research provider availability, pricing, webhook reliability, legal status in Spain, refunds and reconciliation with current sources.
3. Compare PSP cards/wallets, Bizum, SEPA Instant/PIS, XRPL/XRP/RLUSD and digital euro using identical scenarios.
4. Keep XRP/RLUSD and experimental rails OFF until regulated integration and EUR settlement are proven.
5. Prototype behind provider adapters and feature flags, with sandbox-first tests.
6. Report metrics: taps, time to checkout, time to confirmed payment, time to merchant IBAN, failures and effective cost.
7. Never activate live payment methods, spend money or deploy changes without explicit approval.
